Qual das expressões é equivalente a $$\dfrac{\left(\dfrac{3x}{2}\right)}{\left(\dfrac{3}{x}+2\right)}$$?:: $$\dfrac{3x}{2} \cdot \dfrac{1}{\dfrac{3 + 2x}{x}} = \dfrac{3x}{2} \cdot \dfrac{x}{3 + 2x}=\dfrac{3x \cdot x}{2(3 + 2x)} = \dfrac{3x^2}{2(3 + 2x)}$$

Qual das expressões é equivalente a $$\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{xy}}{\dfrac{1}{x}-\dfrac{1}{y}}$$::$$\dfrac{\dfrac{1}{xy}}{\dfrac{y - x}{xy}} = \dfrac{1}{xy} \cdot \dfrac{xy}{y - x}= \dfrac{1}{y - x}$$
